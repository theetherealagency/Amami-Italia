## Rejected, and why

- **LonghornSteakhouse porterhouse** — the plate carries another restaurant's
  trademark.
- **Vinsanto Santorini bottle** — a real bottle, but a Greek producer. Placing it
  beside "Castelgrave Vinsanto, Tuscany" would misrepresent what is being sold.
- **QODE-watermarked theme demo images** already in `uploads/` (`home-3-img-*`,
  `main-home-img-9`) — visible watermark, licensed to the theme only.

Bottle art on `/drinks/` is therefore drawn (SVG), not photographic: every stock
bottle found carries a different producer's label, which would mislabel the wines
Amami actually pours.
